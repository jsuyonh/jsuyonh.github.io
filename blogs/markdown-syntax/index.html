Data Analytics: Turning Data into Better Decisions

Data analytics is an essential tool for businesses because it helps them understand their performance, identify opportunities, and make better decisions based on facts rather than assumptions.

My journey into data analytics began with my interest in numbers and my background in Industrial Engineering. Over the years, I gained experience in finance, administration, commercial operations, and logistics, which helped me understand how businesses work.

In 2019, I discovered Business Analytics and realized I could combine my business knowledge with my passion for analyzing data and solving problems.

Since then, I have been developing my skills in data analytics and Business Intelligence, especially using Power BI to transform data into meaningful reports and interactive dashboards.

For me, data analytics is not just about creating charts or reports. It is about understanding business needs, finding useful insights, and communicating information clearly to support decision-making.

My goal is to turn data into insights that help businesses make better decisions and improve their results.**
